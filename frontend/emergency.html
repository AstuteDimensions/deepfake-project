What Should You Do?

  • Do not panic.
  • Verify the sender using another communication method.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, or banking information.
  • Save screenshots, videos, or links as evidence.
  • Block suspicious accounts.
  • Report the content on the platform where it appeared.
  • Inform trusted family members if impersonation is involved.

Common Deepfake Scams

  • Fake celebrity investment promotions.
  • Voice cloning requesting urgent money transfers.
  • Video impersonation of family members.
  • Romance scams using AI-generated identities.
  • Blackmail using manipulated images or videos.
